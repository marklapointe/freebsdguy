# Math for the Rest of Us

Not every post needs a derivative. When one does, MDWeb can render math with KaTeX.

## Inline

The Gaussian integral feels friendlier as $e^{-x^2}$ than as a hand-wavy “bell curve.”

## Display

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

## A system that fits on a napkin

$$
\begin{aligned}
E &= mc^2 \\
F &= ma
\end{aligned}
$$

If the formulas look sharp, thank the renderer — not the author’s handwriting.
