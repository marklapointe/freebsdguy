# CRT Dreams & Amber Glow

Some people want a clean SaaS landing page. We want **scanlines**, **amber text**, and a color palette that remembers when RAM was measured in kilobytes.

## Pick a skin, keep your files

MDWeb themes are JSON packs — Miami Cyberpunk, Matrix green, Win95 beige, Game Boy olive — applied with CSS variables. Your posts stay Markdown on disk. Switch the pack in **Admin → Appearance**; visitors can flip **light / dark** of that pack in the navbar without changing the site theme.

## A short manifesto

> Own the box. Own the files. Rent nothing you can `pkg install`.

If that sounds like FreeBSD energy: good. That is the point.

## Try this at home

1. Log in as admin  
2. Open **Appearance**  
3. Click **CRT Amber** or **Miami Cyberpunk**  
4. Hit **Set as site theme**  
5. Toggle the sun/moon icon — same DNA, different surface  

Welcome to the glow.
